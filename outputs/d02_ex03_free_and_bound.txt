P(n): n is even

  P(n)                 -- a predicate. n is free, and there is no truth value.
  for all n in 0..9    -- a statement. Its value is False.
  there exists n       -- a statement. Its value is True, witnessed by 0.

the letter n means something different in each line. In the first it stands for
whatever the reader supplies. In the other two it stands for nothing at all: the
quantifier has used it up, and replacing every n by m changes no meaning.

that is why the two statements below say the same thing:
   for all n in 0..9, n is even : False
   for all m in 0..9, m is even : False
