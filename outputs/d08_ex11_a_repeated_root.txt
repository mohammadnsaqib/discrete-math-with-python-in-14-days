a(n) = 4*a(n-1) - 4*a(n-2), a(0) = 1, a(1) = 6
the characteristic equation is x**2 - 4x + 4, whose roots are ['2']
the first twelve terms: [1, 6, 20, 56, 144, 352, 832, 1920, 4352, 9728, 21504, 47104]

the usual shape is A*r**n + B*s**n with two DIFFERENT roots. Here r and s are the
same number, so that shape collapses to (A + B) * 2**n, which is one constant
wearing two names. One constant cannot match two starting terms.
